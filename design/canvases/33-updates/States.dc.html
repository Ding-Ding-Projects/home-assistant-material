How the bar is drawn

Solid: jobs that finished. The share is jobs done out of all jobs in the build.
Striped: jobs running now. The stripes move slowly; with reduced motion they stay still.
Track: jobs still waiting.
Red: the job that failed. The finished part turns grey so nothing reads as success.
Every state also says its meaning in words beside the bar, so colour is never the only signal.
