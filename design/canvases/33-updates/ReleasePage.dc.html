How to update to build 457

  1. In your Home Assistant Material, open Settings, then System, then Updates.
  2. Press Check now. Core 2026.11.1.457 appears in the list.
  3. Choose Update. A backup is offered first when the update supports it.

Core, Supervisor and the operating system are never updated automatically. You choose when.
