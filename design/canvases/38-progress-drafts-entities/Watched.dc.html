RULES
1Both switches are on by default. Anything the camera is asked to show is kept: by a person, a dashboard, another device or an automation.
2Keeping a live view never costs a second live view from the camera service: the same one is watched and kept.
3While a live view is being kept, the picture says so. Nothing is recorded silently.
4A dashboard picture that refreshes all day would fill the disk: repeating snapshots are kept once a minute at most, and only when the picture changed.
5If it cannot be kept, its row says why. It is never left out of the list.
6A camera already recorded all day keeps nothing twice: the row points at the recording.
7Live video starts by itself whenever the camera is on screen, and stops soon after it leaves the screen. Every moment it can be seen is kept.
8When the camera service says "too many requests", the picture shows the wait and the last picture, and live video starts again by itself when the wait is over.
