RULES
1Inside a button, tab or chip the two languages stack: English, then Cantonese smaller beneath. One row of controls, 44 px targets, never taller than 52 px.
2In running text, a state or a label is "English · Cantonese" on one line and ends with an ellipsis when it does not fit. The full text is the accessible name.
3Short range chips stay short: 24 h, 7 d, 30 d. The long form is the accessible name.
4A row, tile or bar is the same height in all three modes. Names that people gave their things are never translated.
5No word is ever split across lines. Numbers, units and times are written once.
6Changing the mode changes the pages already open, with no reload. English mode looks exactly as it did before.
