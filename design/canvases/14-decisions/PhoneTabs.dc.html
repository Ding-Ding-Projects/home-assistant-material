Open pages on a phone, with one hand

Audit 2: how open pages are shown, switched and closed on a phone.

What this board settles

  • Pages opens a sheet from the bottom: a search of the open pages (with the pattern workbench button) and New tab first, then the open pages as cards, two to a row, with Close others.
  • Searching narrows the cards as you type; Close matching and Close the rest act on the result, with Undo.
  • A page closes with its close button or a sideways swipe; Undo stays 5 seconds.

What this board settles

  • Switching without the sheet: swipe the top bar left or right, or use its arrow buttons; never a swipe on the page body, which belongs to cards and sliders.
  • The order is the same as the tab bar on a wide window.
