Two-step confirmation on a phone

Audit 5: about 15 destructive actions use a single confirm. Each gets these two steps.

What this board settles

  • Step 1 lists what will happen. Step 2 asks for the name of the thing (typed) or, where there is no name, a 2 second hold.
  • The error colour appears only on the final button of step 2.

What this board settles

  • Applies to: energy sources, Matter fabrics, Thread reset, turning off a second sign-in step, deleting application credentials, and the other places in the audit list.
  • Screen readers: the hold also accepts a long press of Enter or Space, announced as "hold for 2 seconds".
