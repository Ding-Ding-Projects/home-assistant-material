Search, accessibility and New tab on a phone

Audit 1: below 700 px there is no tab bar, so these could not be reached by touch. Decision: a bottom bar.

What this board settles

  • Bottom bar below 700 px: Home, Search, Pages (with the count of open pages), Access, Menu. The rail and the top tab bar are not drawn at that width.
  • Menu opens the drawer with every destination, Developer tools and Settings, the same order as the rail.
  • New tab is the first row of the Pages sheet (next board), not a sixth button.

What this board settles

  • The bar hides while the keyboard is up and while scrolling down; it returns on any scroll up.
  • Cantonese labels are two characters each and fit; Both mode shows the English label only, with the Cantonese in the accessible name.
