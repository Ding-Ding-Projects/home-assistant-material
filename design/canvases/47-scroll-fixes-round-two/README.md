# Canvas 47: scroll fixes and polish, round two

Round two of the frontend survey (25 November 2026), run after the canvas 46 fixes were merged and told to skip everything round one found. One reviewer read each place in the source and a second reviewer tried to disprove it; 48 of 58 held up, and every one of them has a home on a board below. One canvas page per area, a title note per page and a note beside every board.

35 boards: 28 phone boards at 390 x 844, 1 landscape phone board, and 6 fluid wide boards at 1280 px.

Every board has a `before` stage that reproduces the problem as it is in today's build, marked "Today", and a default stage that shows the fix. Every search field on these boards has the pattern workbench beside it and it works on the board; boards with a search have `pattern` and `pattern-invalid` stages.

## Tweaks

Every board declares the same four Tweaks: `accent` (colour: `#98CBFF`, `#9BD6A4`, `#F2C744`, `#D4BEE6`; default `#98CBFF`), `motion` (on or off; default on), `theme` (`dark` or `light`; default `dark`) and `stage` (that board's stages, always including `before`; default the fix). Reduced motion stops every animation whatever the motion switch says.

## Rules the boards carry

The rules of [canvas 42](../42-modern-polish-2/README.md) and [canvas 46](../46-scroll-fixes-and-polish/README.md) hold: today's look with a modern polish, dashboard cards stay as they are, one scroller per page, nothing wider than the screen from 320 px, page ends clear every fixed or floating thing, text wraps or has its full text reachable, sheets keep title and buttons on screen, 48 px targets, a pattern workbench beside every search, 24-hour times. Icons are copied from the Material Design Icons package; sample data is made up; no remote fonts, images or scripts.

Every finding, with its exact lines, its effect at phone and desktop width and the agreed fix, is in [`findings.md`](findings.md).

## State

Design only; nothing on these boards is built yet. A reviewer read every page against the rules and the findings and the boards were corrected where it found problems; some pages were also opened in a headless browser by the designers.
