# Canvas 46: scroll fixes and polish

Boards for the scroll, clipping and dated-look problems found in a survey of the frontend on 25 November 2026, one canvas page per area. Each finding was read in the source by one reviewer and re-checked by a second reviewer who tried to disprove it; 56 of 59 held up and every one of them has a home on a board below. Each page has a title note, and every board has its own note beside it saying what it shows, what to try and what is not built yet.

36 boards: 28 phone boards at 390 x 844 and 8 fluid wide boards at 1280 px.

Every board has a `before` stage that reproduces the problem as it is in today's build, marked with a small "Today" tag, and a default stage that shows the fix. Flip the `stage` tweak to compare.

Every search field on these boards has the pattern workbench button beside it, as every search in the app does. The button opens the workbench for that field (a bottom sheet on a phone, a popover on a wide window) with the pattern, flags, a plain-words reading, a live count of the board's own rows that match, and Use pattern; boards with a search have `pattern` and `pattern-invalid` stages.

## Rules the boards carry

The rules of [canvas 42](../42-modern-polish-2/README.md) still hold: today's look with a modern polish, dashboard cards stay as they are, one scroller per page, nothing wider than the screen from 320 px, the end of a page clears every fixed or floating thing, text wraps or ends in three dots with the full text reachable, sheets keep their title and buttons on screen, every target is 48 px or has a 48 px hit area. Every search field has its pattern workbench. Times are 24-hour. Colours are the Material Design 3 tokens from `design/source/m3.css`, copied into each board so a board stays one self-contained page; no remote fonts, images or scripts. Sample data is made up.

The survey that found these problems, with the exact lines, the effect at phone and desktop width and the agreed fix for each, is in [`findings.md`](findings.md).
