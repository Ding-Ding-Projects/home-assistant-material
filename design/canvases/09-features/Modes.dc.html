Focus modes

Five separate switches. Each one changes how screens behave, nothing more. They are not medical tools.

FocusShows the one area you are working in and dims the rest.
Low stimulationStill pictures, quieter colour, no sound cues. Works with your device's reduced motion setting.
Time awarenessShows the time, how long a task has run, and what is next.
One thing at a timeOpens one step or one card at a time, with a clear next.
MomentumKeeps your place and offers the next small step when you come back.
No streaks, scores or praise. With School mode on, Low stimulation stays available and playfulness stays off.
