Guidance note in setup dialogs, on a phone

A collapsed note per step: what the step is for, what is needed first, a link to the documentation.

What this board settles

  • Collapsed by default, one row. It remembers being opened for the rest of that setup.
  • Three parts in a fixed order: what it is for, needed first, documentation link.

Works differently from today's build

  • On a phone the note opens as a sheet instead of expanding in place, so the form does not move down.
  • Steps that have no guidance show no row at all, rather than an empty note.
