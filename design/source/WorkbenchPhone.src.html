Pattern workbench on a phone

Below 700 px the workbench takes the whole visible screen and follows the keyboard. Order from the top: title, mode, pattern field with its flags chip, sections, content, footer.

What this board settles

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Order: title bar, Text or Pattern switch, the pattern field, the section tabs, the content, then the footer.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The eight flags become one chip beside the Text or Pattern switch, showing the letters that are on. It opens a sheet of eight switches.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Three section tabs fit at 320 px: Build, Test, Replace. Test cases, Performance, Use in code, Library and Engine sit under More, which shows the chosen one's name when one of them is open. Nothing scrolls sideways and nothing is cut.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Footer: one line of note, then a More button holding Copy and Export on the left, Cancel and Use pattern on the right. It never wraps.

Works differently from today's build

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The flag switches move out of the header into the field chip and its sheet.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The sideways-scrolling tab row is replaced by three tabs and More; Copy and Export move under the footer's More button.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@While the keyboard is up the footer hides; it comes back when the keyboard closes. Use stays reachable from the keyboard's Enter key.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Cantonese: tab names are shorter, so more tabs would fit; keep three for one layout in every language.
