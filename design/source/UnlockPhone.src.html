Unlock prompt and PIN keypad on a phone

A full-width sheet with a keypad of large keys. More than one credential is asked in order, shown as steps.

What this board settles

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Key order is 1 to 9, then Clear, 0, Backspace. With Shuffle on, the ten digits change places each time the prompt opens; Clear and Backspace never move.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Typing is always allowed: "Type instead" swaps the keypad for a field and the keyboard, and back.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The wait after wrong tries is 5 seconds, then 15, then 1 minute, then 5 minutes, counted down on the note.

Works differently from today's build

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Cancel and Submit sit side by side under the keypad at equal width, rather than in a footer that the keyboard can push off screen.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The recovery line appears only after a wrong try, so the first view is calm.
