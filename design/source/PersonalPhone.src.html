Personal settings on a phone

Grouped by task, searchable, every row explains itself. The preview shows what the language and playfulness choices do without making the page look broken.

What this board settles

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Three groups: Language and playfulness, Sound, Motion and attention. On a phone they are chips that scroll the page to the group.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Each row: title and control on one line, the explanation under it. Opening a row adds the reach line, the default and Reset.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Reset all lives in the top bar's More menu and asks once, listing what changes.

Works differently from today's build

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The "what it reaches today" line is shown only when a row is opened, so the page does not read as a list of disclaimers.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Language mode and both playfulness levels say "Preview only" once, on the preview card, in the amber tone used for "not yet" everywhere.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Cantonese and Both: the preview grows to two lines; the rows keep their size.
