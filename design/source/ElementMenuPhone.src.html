Element tools on a phone, opened by touch and hold

On a phone the menu of an element is a bottom sheet, not a menu anchored to the finger. A hold is shown as it happens.

What this board settles

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Hold for 600 ms. A ring fills around the touch point from the first 150 ms, so a hold is not mistaken for a scroll; at 600 ms the phone buzzes once (where vibration is allowed) and the sheet opens.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The sheet starts at its content height and names the element at its top, so the person knows which one they held.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Typing in the sheet search expands it to the full height above the keyboard; clearing the search lets it settle back.

Works differently from today's build

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Today the menu opens anchored at the touch point and the keyboard can cover its search. On a phone it becomes this sheet.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The long-press threshold stays 600 ms, but moving the finger more than 10 px cancels it, so scrolling never opens the menu.
