Lock wizard on a phone

Beside the element on a wide window, the wizard becomes a full-height sheet on a phone. A segmented line and the step name show progress.

What this board settles

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Six steps: what to lock, what unlocks it, create each credential in order, how long an unlock lasts, the disclosure and recovery line, confirm.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Progress: a six-segment line under the title, and "Step 3 of 6, Create each one" as the title's second line. Read out on every step change.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Back is a text button on the left; the main button on the right is Next, then Create lock on the last step.

Works differently from today's build

  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@On a phone the anchored panel becomes a full-height sheet from the bottom; the element stays named in the title.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@The acknowledgement checkbox moves from the confirm step to step 5, beside the words it acknowledges.
  • @@I:CheckCircle.s@@Cantonese: the step name can need two lines; the title bar grows rather than truncating it.
